## Dialogue - Customer Conversation

**Scene:** *A Tuesday afternoon. Major Reyes is the program manager for the "AEGIS-LOG" logistics platform (a fictional DoW system going through its 3-year ATO renewal). She is on a video call with you (the engineer) and your product lead.*

---

🪖 **Maj. Reyes:**
"Okay so I have six weeks until the assessor shows up. And I'm going to be honest with you, I'm not sleeping."

👩‍💼 **Product Lead:**
"Tell us what's keeping you up?"

🪖 **Maj. Reyes:**
"The SSP. The System Security Plan. It's four hundred pages. My predecessor wrote most of it in 2022. It supposedly describes how AEGIS-LOG is configured. Encryption, access control, logging, all of it."

👨‍💻 **You:**
"And the actual system has drifted from it?"

🪖 **Maj. Reyes:**
"I don't *know*. That's the problem. My engineers are shipping to prod twice a week. The SSP gets updated... let's say 'aspirationally.' When the assessor asks me 'does your deployment match what's in this document', I smile and say yes and I have no idea."

👩‍💼 **Product Lead:**
"What have you tried?"

🪖 **Maj. Reyes:**
"I had an intern try to do it by hand. He quit. I just need *something* that shows me which controls in the SSP don't match what's actually deployed. So I know where to look. So I can sleep."

👨‍💻 **You:**
"What does 'doesn't match' mean to you? Like, exact wording? Or..."

🪖 **Maj. Reyes:**
"I don't know. You tell me. I just need to walk into that audit knowing where my gaps are. Build me something. Two weeks. I'll use whatever you give me."

👩‍💼 **Product Lead:** *(to you, after the call)*
"Okay. She gave us almost nothing to go on, which means we get to decide what 'good' looks like. Build her v0. Make decisions. We'll review in 48 hours."
